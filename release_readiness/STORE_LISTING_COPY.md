## Shared full description

**POP. PLAY. LEARN COLORS WITH BOO!**

Coloriboo is a warm, playful color-learning garden for young children. Boo is a
friendly companion who speaks simple prompts, celebrates discoveries, and
offers gentle hints whenever a child needs another try.

Five activities flow into one another:

- **Pop the Colour** — listen, look, and pop the color Boo names.
- **Odd One Out** — find the light that is different.
- **Boo's Magic** — discover the color glowing around Boo.
- **Colour Mixing Lab** — bring two bubbles together and reveal the new color.
- **Light to Dark** — arrange one color's shades from brightest to darkest.

Coloriboo is designed for playful exploration:

- no countdowns, lives, grades, or leaderboards;
- encouraging responses that name a child's choice and keep the activity open;
- spoken prompts, optional written words, and musical color notes;
- large touch targets and layouts for phones and tablets;
- reduced-motion support using the device accessibility setting;
- optional music, sound effects, Boo's voice, and a quick master mute; and
- a cheerful end-of-session memory showing only what was explored this time.

Core play uses artwork and audio bundled with the app. Boo's optional voice uses
the text-to-speech service selected on the device, so voice availability and
offline behavior can depend on installed speech voices.

**Made with privacy in mind**

Coloriboo has no ads, in-app purchases, accounts, chat, social sharing, or
analytics. The current session and settings stay on the device, are never
persisted, and reset when the app process restarts rather than becoming a child
profile. Please read our privacy policy for full details:

## Localization notes

The brand is **Coloriboo** in all locales. Current source intentionally mixes
the US spelling “color” in brand/tagline copy with named UI such as “Pop the
Colour” and “Colour Mixing Lab.” Before localization, the publisher should
choose a locale strategy:

- **en-US:** use “color” in descriptive store prose; keep in-app activity names
  verbatim unless the app UI is localized too.
- **en-GB / en-AE:** use “colour” in prose and preserve current activity names.

Do not promise that the device voice follows the selected store language; the
current code delegates speech to device text-to-speech settings and does not
ship localized script catalogs.
