## About Coloriboo

Coloriboo is a color-learning app for young children. Children play short
activities with Boo, including matching colors, finding a different color,
mixing colors, and arranging shades from light to dark.

This policy describes the data practices of the Coloriboo mobile application.
It does not describe the independent practices of the Apple App Store, Google
Play, a device manufacturer, or a speech-engine provider.

## Information Coloriboo collects

The current version of Coloriboo does **not** ask for, collect, transmit, sell,
rent, or share personal information.

In particular, Coloriboo has:

- no child or parent account;
- no sign-in, profile, name, email, phone number, or address field;
- no advertising or advertising identifier use;
- no analytics, tracking, attribution, or crash-reporting service;
- no in-app purchases or payment collection;
- no chat, social sharing, user-generated content, or third-party links in the
  child play experience; and
- no access to location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

## Learning activity and settings data

Coloriboo keeps a small amount of activity state on the device while the app is
open. This includes the current activity, colors explored during the current
play session, completed activity counts, and sound or word-display choices.

This state is used only to run the app and show the current session summary. It
is held in application memory, is not sent to the publisher or another server,
and is not stored as a child profile. Settings are never written to persistent
storage and reset when the app process is terminated or restarted. Session
information is also cleared when the child returns home or starts a new session.

## Audio and speech

Coloriboo plays sound effects and music bundled inside the app. The audio
library may copy those bundled files into the app's temporary storage so they
can be played efficiently. Those files contain no child or parent information.
The operating system may clear temporary files according to its own storage
rules.

If Boo's voice is enabled, Coloriboo asks the device's text-to-speech service to
speak fixed learning prompts, color names, and encouragement written into the
app. Coloriboo does not record a child's voice and does not accept free-form
speech or text. The selected device speech engine may work entirely on-device
or may use a network-based voice according to the family's device and speech
provider settings. Any processing performed independently by that provider is
governed by the provider's terms and privacy policy; the Coloriboo publisher
does not receive the spoken text or resulting audio.

Parents can turn Boo's voice, music, and sound effects off in the app.

## Children's privacy

Coloriboo is designed to avoid collecting personal information from children.
The current app does not provide a way for a child to submit personal
information to the publisher. If you believe a child has sent personal
information to us outside the app, contact us at [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]. We
will investigate and delete information when required.

Parents and guardians should review the app and device settings before use.

## Third-party software and stores

Coloriboo is built with Flutter and uses software libraries for local audio and
device text-to-speech. The current app code does not configure those libraries
for advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, or developer-operated network
services.

Apple, Google, device manufacturers, and operating-system service providers may
process information independently when a parent downloads the app, manages the
device, uses a network-backed speech voice, or contacts a store. Their own
privacy notices apply to those activities.

## Data retention and deletion

Coloriboo does not maintain a server database of users or personal information,
so there is no Coloriboo account or cloud record to delete. Terminating or
restarting the app process clears its in-memory settings and play state; merely
sending the app to the background may leave that process in memory. Uninstalling
the app removes its app-managed local files subject to the operating system's
normal behavior.

For questions about information processed by an app store, device, or speech
provider, contact that provider directly.

## Security

Coloriboo minimizes privacy risk by not asking for personal information and by
keeping current-session gameplay on the device. No method of software or device
storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, but the app does not operate a
remote service that stores a child's gameplay or identity.
